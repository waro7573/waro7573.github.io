## 💡 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you experience problems, check these items:

*   **No Sound:** Check that you granted the application permission to access your microphone in Windows Privacy Settings.
*   **Updates Fail:** Ensure you have enough disk space and a stable internet connection. The app checks for new versions automatically upon startup.
*   **Agent Not Responding:** Verify that your coding agent (such as Claude Code) is running and accessible in your workspace directory.
*   **Application Won't Start:** Right-click the shortcut and select "Run as administrator."
